# Chapter 09: Research Fan-out

## What We'll Cover
- Fanning out read-only researchers
- Synthesizing multiple briefs
- Avoiding conflicting assumptions
- Cost vs. depth trade-offs

## Why Fan-out Works for Reads
Reading is parallelizable. Each researcher reads a different file or searches a different topic.

## Synthesis
The Lead Engineer receives a merged brief with findings, conflicts, and unknowns.

## Exercise
Build a 2-researcher fan-out for a codebase query.

## Key Takeaway
Parallel reads increase coverage. Synthesis prevents contradictions.
