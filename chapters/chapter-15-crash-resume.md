# Chapter 15: Surviving Crashes — Kill and Resume

## What We'll Cover
- Crash-resume as a first-class property
- Demo: kill the worker mid-mission
- Proving no work is lost
- Proving no tokens are re-spent

## The Kill-and-Resume Test
Start a mission, kill the worker after some cycles, restart it, and verify it resumes exactly where it left off.

## Exercise
Run the LRA demo mission, kill the worker, restart it, and capture the mission status.

## Key Takeaway
If you cannot demonstrate kill-and-resume, you do not have a durable agent.
